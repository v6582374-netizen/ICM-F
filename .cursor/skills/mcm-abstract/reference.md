# MCM Summary Sheet (Abstract) Rules

The abstract is the primary filter for judges. It must be a standalone document.

## Structure Checklist

1. **Restatement**: Begin with a brief restatement of the problem in your own words (do not copy the prompt).
2. **Approach**: Explicitly state the modeling techniques used (e.g., "We constructed a differential equation model...", "Using a Monte Carlo simulation...").
3. **Results**: You MUST include specific numerical results and key conclusions. Vague statements like "results were promising" are prohibited.
4. **Sensitivity**: Mention the validation or sensitivity analysis performed.

## Style Constraints

- **No Math Symbols**: Avoid complex mathematical notation in the abstract unless absolutely necessary. Use text descriptions.
- **Hook**: The first sentence must be engaging and relevant to the real-world context.
- **Length**: Must fit strictly on one page.

## Comparison Example

- **Weak**: "We built a model to solve the problem and found good results."
- **Strong**: "We developed a multi-objective optimization model based on Genetic Algorithms. Our simulation indicates that the optimal strategy reduces costs by 15.4% while maintaining service levels above 98%."
