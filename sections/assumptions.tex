\section{Assumptions and Notation}
\subsection{Fundamental Assumptions}

To ensure the reproducibility and auditability of our model, we formalize the core premises of the modeling process as follows:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.4em]

\item \textbf{Baseline Task Structure Stability Assumption}. \textit{Justification:} When $A(t)=0$, the task structure remains at baseline; that is, its endogenous changes are stationary. This simplification allows us to isolate the effect of GenAI adoption from other confounding factors in occupational evolution.

\item \textbf{Monotonic Adoption Rate Assumption}. \textit{Justification:} The current GenAI adoption rate increases monotonically from $0$ to $1$, characterizing the staged penetration during the technology diffusion process. Historical data on technology adoption (e.g., internet, mobile devices) consistently shows S-curve patterns with monotonic growth phases.

\end{itemize}
